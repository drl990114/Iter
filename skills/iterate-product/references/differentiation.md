# Compare opportunities

Read research, the saved choice, charter, [opportunity-rubric.md](opportunity-rubric.md), and [opportunities-schema.md](opportunities-schema.md).

For an open-ended choice, present 2–3 distinct concrete improvements. A single explicitly selected opportunity is valid. Existing 3–7 item reports remain usable. Reuse the opening proposals and improve their evidence instead of restarting discovery.

Each opportunity needs a specific problem, evidence, a falsifiable hypothesis, alternative gap, counterargument, smallest testable scope, and bounded risks.

Score with the unchanged rubric and `score` command. Preserve script-generated scores and ranks, and keep `selected_id` separate from ranking. A different winner does not override the user's choice. Surface material contradictions with consequences; do not silently switch implementation.

Write `02-opportunities.json`, validate, and continue to the selected experiment. Neither a high rank nor a local task pass proves market differentiation.
